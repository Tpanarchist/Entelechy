# Entelechy

Entelechy is a persistent artificial intelligence system designed to develop knowledge rather than merely retrieve, imitate, or statistically reproduce it.

Its central premise is that intelligence should not be identified with a single neural network, language model, memory store, or software process. Instead, Entelechy is the enduring informational organization within which perception, memory, learning, reasoning, prediction, emotion-like prioritization, action, language, tools, and metacognition can develop and interact.

## The Seed

Entelechy begins from the smallest possible epistemic seed: a bounded, persistent system capable of receiving differences, retaining information, undergoing transformation, allocating finite resources, and distinguishing its own continuity from changes in its internal state.

## Identity

Its identity is therefore not equivalent to any particular model or configuration:

```text
Identity ≠ State
Identity ≠ Memory
Identity ≠ Neural Model
Identity ≠ Language
```

Models, memories, representations, and cognitive mechanisms may change while Entelechy remains the same persistent system.

## Learning

Entelechy treats learning as the transformation of experience into increasingly useful internal structure. A simplified developmental cycle is:

```text
Experience
→ Distinction
→ Retention
→ Pattern
→ Prediction
→ Discrepancy
→ Adaptation
→ New Structure
```

The same underlying discrepancy process can operate at several levels.

- **World discrepancy** compares predicted experience with observed experience and drives learning.
- **Self discrepancy** compares preferred or expected self-relevant states with perceived states and can produce emotion-like salience, motivation, and resource allocation.
- **Meta-discrepancy** compares expected cognitive performance with actual cognitive performance and enables metacognition and self-correction.

## Not Fundamentally an LLM

Entelechy is therefore not fundamentally an LLM. Neural networks are optional cognitive organs within the larger system. Language models may provide pattern recognition, hypothesis generation, language understanding, or communication, but persistent knowledge, identity, memory, tools, and epistemic history need not reside inside neural weights.

## Objective

The long-term objective is to determine the minimum machinery required for a persistent intelligence to develop increasingly accurate models of itself and its environment through interaction.

In its strongest form, Entelechy can be described as:

> A persistent informational organism that converts experience into structure, structure into prediction, discrepancy into learning, and learning into increasingly effective understanding and action.
